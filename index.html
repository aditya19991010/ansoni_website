01About

I am a PhD student in Bioinformatics at National Taiwan University, working in the UFO Lab under Prof. C.-K. Sun. My doctoral research applies genome-wide association studies (GWAS) to age-related traits.

Before NTU, I modelled epigenetic drift in histone modifications during ageing as a Junior Research Fellow at IBAB, Bengaluru, and ran expression and eQTL analyses on a maternal-nutrition birth cohort at CSIR-CCMB, Hyderabad. My MSc work at CDFD produced a genome-wide catalogue of NLR disease-resistance genes in Panicum miliaceum.

I am drawn to the point where computation meets clinical need, an interest that began in the hospital wards where my mother worked as a nurse. I want the models I build to make early disease prediction more accessible.
